The APR folds in negotiation fees, service charges, excise duty and credit-life insurance. Two loans at the same 13% rate can have very different true costs, always compare APR.

To calculate the simplified annual percentage rate (APR) including total finance fees, we use:

$$\text{APR} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Fees} + \text{Total Interest}}{\text{Principal}} \right) / N \times 365 \times 100\%$$

where $N$ is the number of days in the loan term.
