title: "APR Is Not the Interest Rate"

---
The APR includes negotiation fees, service charges, excise duty and credit-life insurance. Therefore two loans at the same 13% rate can have very different APR. APR is given by:

$$\text{APR} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Fees} + \text{Total Interest}}{\text{Principal}} \right) / N \times 365 \times 100\%$$

where $N$ is the number of days in the loan term.
